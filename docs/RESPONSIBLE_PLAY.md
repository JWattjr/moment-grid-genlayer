# Responsible play and real-value launch policy

Moment Grid currently uses testnet GEN with no promised cash value. A real-value
release must not be presented as merely a contract deployment.

Before any such release, the product needs legal review in every served
jurisdiction, independent security and economic audits, age and location
controls, sanctions screening where required, self-exclusion, deposit/loss/time
limits, cooling-off periods, reality checks, clear maximum-loss confirmation,
and local problem-gambling support links.

Product copy must never imply guaranteed profit, recovery of losses, urgency to
stake, or that a local replay is a payout. The contract's finalized position is
the only authoritative result. Underfilled, unsupported, and timed-out rounds
must expose their refund action without requiring operator cooperation.
